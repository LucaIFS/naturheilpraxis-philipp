About me

Egon Philipp, Heilpraktiker

The marvel of the human body and the possibilities of healing in a natural way have fascinated me for as long as I can remember. That is why I decided to become a Heilpraktiker — and finally found my place.

Because I wanted a profession with close contact to people, I began training as a paramedic in 1997. Empathy and great responsibility were fundamental at every stage of my career in the emergency services — and yet I felt that my true calling lay elsewhere. The search led me to naturopathy.
