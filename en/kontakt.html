Will my health insurance cover the costs?

German statutory health insurance does not usually cover Heilpraktiker services. Private health insurance and supplementary Heilpraktiker policies reimburse the costs in full or in part depending on your plan — it is best to check with your insurer in advance. You will receive an invoice according to the German fee schedule for Heilpraktiker (GebüH). International insurance plans may differ — please check your policy.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Helpful items include existing medical findings, recent lab results and a list of your medication. You can fill in the consent form (in German) in advance and bring it with you — it saves time during the appointment.

How quickly can I get an appointment?

Appointments are by arrangement. Simply send me two preferred dates by email or WhatsApp — you will promptly receive a confirmation or an alternative suggestion.

What if I need to cancel an appointment?

No problem — please cancel at least 24 hours in advance, in writing or by phone. Appointments cancelled later or missed must be charged at my regular hourly rate.
