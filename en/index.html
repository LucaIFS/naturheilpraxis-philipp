Allergies

Up to 20 percent of Germans now suffer from an allergy — a misregulation of the immune system that naturopathy can help address.

In an allergy, the immune system reacts against substances that are actually harmless. From a naturopathic perspective, burdens on the connective tissue play a major role — heavy metals, environmental toxins, medication or intestinal imbalances build up in the body as waste does in a bin, until it overflows: that is when the illness breaks out.

Treatment

It starts with finding the cause. Long-term treatment focuses on detoxification and modulation — for example with potentised autologous blood — restoring the gut and resolving deficiencies.

Back pain

From lumbago to chronic tension: chiropractic, Baunscheidt therapy and Bowen therapy used in combination.

Lumbago usually strikes without warning: sharp pain in the lower back, often triggered by everyday sudden movements while bending, lifting or turning. Possible causes include blocked vertebral joints, disc changes or strained muscles and joint capsules.

Treatment

In the acute phase the focus is on pain relief, warmth and rest — with manual methods such as chiropractic and massage. Once the acute symptoms subside, the rule is: get moving again as soon as possible.

Skin conditions

Chronic skin problems such as neurodermatitis are on the rise — especially among children and teenagers. Holistic care instead of treating symptoms only.

The skin expresses and excretes what happens in our metabolism — it is not called the "third kidney" for nothing. Skin symptoms therefore often point to underlying organ and metabolic issues, for example in the gut or the liver.

Treatment

Rather than treating only from the outside, therapy works "from within": finding the cause, restoring the gut, detoxification and modulation — for example with potentised autologous blood.

Exhaustion & burn-out

When work, family and everyday life drain your energy: regeneration and strengthening of your own resources.

Performance pressure, relationship problems and existential worries keep the body in a state of constant alert. Nervousness, exhaustion, sleeplessness or low mood are often just the beginning of a cycle that, untreated, can develop into burn-out.

Treatment

Following the principles of regulatory therapy: identify and reduce stress factors, learn active relaxation (yoga, autogenic training), exercise in fresh air — and resolve deficiencies of vitamins B1, B6, B12 and folic acid. Acupuncture can provide additional balance.

Choline citrate infusions

Deep relaxation: choline citrate infusions can support the autonomic nervous system — for inner calm and balance.

Choline citrate is a precursor of the neurotransmitter acetylcholine, which plays a key role in regulating the autonomic nervous system. A balanced nervous system needs the harmonious interplay of the sympathetic (activation) and parasympathetic (relaxation and regeneration) branches.

When can the infusion help?

In cases of inner restlessness, stress and exhaustion, choline citrate can support the parasympathetic system and help the body find its way back into balance — for deep relaxation and restful sleep.

Prevention

Prevent instead of repair

You take your car in for regular servicing.
What about yourself?

Work, family, a thousand obligations — in everyday life, your own needs usually come last. Many people simply keep functioning until nothing works any more. Only when the body goes on strike do they act — and then it should be fixed as quickly as possible.

You can take a car to the garage and have the faulty part replaced. Our body cannot do that: what has been overloaded for years cannot simply be exchanged. There are no spare parts for your body.

This is precisely where naturopathy is strongest: it steps in before a burden becomes an illness — strengthening the body's self-healing powers, recognising warning signs early and bringing body and mind back into balance.

The best time to do something for your health is not when something hurts. It is now.
